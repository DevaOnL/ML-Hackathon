# Development and repository provenance

This project is being developed with AI coding assistance at the account owner’s request. The implementation, experiments, report and tests are produced within this workspace; they are not imported from another team. Team members remain responsible for checking, understanding and defending every submitted claim.

The owner requested repository operations through four already authenticated accounts: `DevaOnL`, `Master-rudra1`, `gowth139am-lab`, and `Gowthamm08`. Commits and pull requests may be distributed across those accounts as an administrative workflow. Account attribution does **not** claim that four humans independently wrote the associated code, and commit times are not backdated or fabricated. No fabricated collaboration history, metrics, screenshots or training curves are used.

Only measured execution results are reported. Per-observation supervision is derived from supplied route geometry because the raw labels contain route arc sequences, not observation-aligned labels; associated metrics are explicitly described as proxy-label metrics. The ten student-test trips are permitted development data under the written guidelines. The user reported ten new evaluator trips after the current three-family model was frozen, tested and deployed. They were not inspected or evaluated for this report. Their separate website section is deferred at the user’s request.

Raw supplied archives, video, organiser documents, credential files, model download caches and reserved evaluator data are excluded from Git. Training/evaluation source, small trained artifacts, experimental records, deployment code and the consolidated report are included.
